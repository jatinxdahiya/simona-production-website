About

An Independent Label For Original Voices

Simona Production is an India-based independent record label built to give artists a platform to release their music with confidence.

We believe in individuality, creativity, and hard work. Our focus is to support original voices and help shape songs that connect with listeners.

If you are a lyricist, singer, composer, or artist looking for a platform to create and release songs, we would love to hear from you.
